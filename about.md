# Mod Previews
**View preview images for mods.**

*by [Cheeseworks](user:6408873)!*

---

## About
This mod adds preview images to Geode mods' information pop-ups if their developer included them!

---

### Viewing
A <cg>small interface</c> will <cy>appear at the bottom of the Geode mod pop-up</c>, where you can view and go through pages of preview images for that mod.

### Developers
To add preview images, <cy>add a `previews/` folder in your repository</c>, to which you will be able to add <cg>up to 10 PNG images</c> to preview your mod! <cr>Each preview image must be named with the following format</c>.

> - `preview-`**`{num}`**`.png`

For example, `preview-4.png`, `preview-10.png`.

#### Supported Platforms
This mod supports fetching images from repositories hosted on <cg>[GitHub](https://github.com/), [GitLab](https://gitlab.com/), and any [Forgejo-based platform](https://forgejo.org/)</c>.
